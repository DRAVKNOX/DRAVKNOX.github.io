        Screens

        Orange rails. Dark cards. Time you can read while barely alive

        The app is not trying to be delicate. It is trying to be readable at 6:30 AM when your soul is still buffering.

        Install

        Grab the APK, or summon it yourself

        Releases live on GitHub. Play testing exists when available. Source is there too, if you prefer building your own tiny time contraption.

        Code

        Kotlin, Canvas, and boring Android parts

        No grand temple of dependencies here. Kotlin, custom Canvas drawing, direct AlarmManager scheduling, and basic Android pieces that do not panic on AOSP forks.

        Privacy

        Nothing to upload, nothing to sell, nothing to explain

        Watch does not need the internet to ring, count down, track laps, or show world clocks. Your alarms stay on your device, not in some server room at the end of time.

        Watch does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.
        Alarms, timers, and world clocks stay on your device without cloud sync.
        Watch does not require internet access and cannot communicate with external servers.
